# hows-your-trip
How’s your trip? is a travel app where friends plan trips together and share real trips with real costs.

A social travel app built around real trips from real travellers. People post their trips like any Instagram or Rednote post, with the budget and route they actually took attached underneath, so others can see what a trip really cost and reuse the plan with one tap. Friends plan together in one shared trip: an AI planner turns everyone’s places into a day-by-day schedule, a Prep tab covers what to know and what to pack, and a Bookings tab keeps every ticket and QR code in one place, showing the real cost and who owes whom. When the trip ends, it becomes a new post, and the traveller earns a stamp.
